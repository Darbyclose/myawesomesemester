## About this course

Automobile Safety looks at how cars are designed to protect people and how injuries happen during crashes. We learn about seat belts, airbags, crash testing, and the safety features developed over time. I am taking this class because it sounded really different from my other classes, and I thought it would be interesting to learn more about something that affects basically everyone who drives or rides in a car.


## About William Weikart

Steven Rowson, Ph.D., is a Professor in Virginia Tech's Department of Biomedical Engineering and Mechanics. He earned his bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Rowan University and his Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Virginia Tech. He is also director of the Virginia Tech Helmet Lab, where he works to make helmets and other protective equipment safer.

### Research interests

Dr. Rowson's research focuses on injury biomechanics and understanding what happens to the human body during impacts. A large part of his research looks at concussions and head injuries and how protective equipment can reduce the risk of injury. His work combines engineering, safety testing, and real-world data to find ways to better protect people.


### Publications

- "Can Helmet Design Reduce the Risk of Concussion in Football?"
- "Brain Injury Prediction: Assessing the Combined Probability of Concussion Using Linear and Rotational Head Acceleration"
- "Development of the STAR Evaluation System for Football Helmets: Integrating Player Head Impact Exposure and Risk of Concussion"

### Service and outreach

Dr. Rowson directs the Virginia Tech Helmet Lab, which tests helmets and provides independent safety ratings to the public. The lab tests helmets used for sports and other activities and makes its ratings available so people can compare how well different helmets protect against injury. His work has also encouraged manufacturers to improve helmet designs and gives consumers research they can use when choosing protective equipment.
