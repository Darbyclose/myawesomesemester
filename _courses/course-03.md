## About this course

Intro to Health Communication examines how communication shapes health and healthcare. We cover topics like how patients and healthcare providers communicate, health campaigns, misinformation, and how the media can shape how people think about health. I am taking this class because it counts toward my Communication major, but I also think health communication affects everyone. I hope to learn more about how good communication can make a difference in people's health decisions.


## About Professor Name

Adrienne Holz is an Associate Professor in Virginia Tech's School of Communication. She teaches and researches topics involving health, media, and communication. Her work combines communication with real-world issues, especially looking at how the information people see in the media can influence their beliefs and behaviors.

### Research interests

Professor Holz's research covers health communication, media effects, misinformation, and persuasive messages. Some of her more recent work has looked at topics like COVID-19 misinformation and vaccines, mask-wearing, and media campaigns surrounding food and sugary drinks. Much of her research examines why people respond to certain messages and how communication can influence health decisions.


### Publications

- "Ideological Consistency and News Sharing as Predictors of Masking Among College Students"
- "A Content Analysis of Persuasive Appeals Used in Media Campaigns to Encourage and Discourage Sugary Beverages and Water in the United States"
- "All the President's Tweets: Effects of Exposure to Trump's 'Fake News' Accusations on Perceptions of Journalists, News Stories, and Issue Evaluation"
- "A Forgotten Sexuality: Content Analysis of Bisexuality in the Medical Literature over Two Decades"


### Service and outreach

Professor Holz is also involved in research and service outside of the classroom. She directs the Virginia Tech Science and Health Communication Lab and is a member of Virginia Tech's Whole Health Consortium. She has also worked on projects studying health misinformation, including research on misinformation surrounding the COVID-19 vaccine.
